# Alexis Medrano

Control and Automation Engineer (IPN ESIME Zacatenco), based in Mexico City.

I maintain and troubleshoot elevators and escalators in the field, and I previously worked on vertical and horizontal transport systems for Metro CDMX, Cablebús and Mexicable. In my own time I build small industrial data pipelines that take PLC signals into databases and dashboards. My goal is to connect what happens on the plant floor with the data maintenance teams use to make decisions.
